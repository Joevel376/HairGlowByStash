Deposit Requirement

A non-refundable deposit of J$2,000 is required to secure all appointments. For services priced below J$2,000, the full service amount is required as the deposit.

Example: A J$1,500 service requires a J$1,500 deposit.

The deposit is applied toward the total cost of your service. Appointments are not confirmed until the deposit has been received.

Appointment time slots are not held without payment.
