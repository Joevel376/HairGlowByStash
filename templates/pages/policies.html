Booking Policies

Please review our policies carefully before scheduling your appointment

To provide the best possible experience for all clients, Hair Glow By Stash has the following booking policies in place. Please review carefully before scheduling an appointment.

Hair Preparation

  • Hair must be freshly washed before your appointment.
  • No oil or leave-in conditioner should be applied prior to arrival.
  • All new wigs must be dropped off at least 2 days before the scheduled appointment.

Salon Guidelines

  • Hair Glow By Stash operates on a one-client-at-a-time basis.
  • No extra company or children are allowed inside the salon during appointments.

Service Information

  • Gel X nail services only.
  • Acrylic soak-offs and acrylic refills are not offered.

By booking an appointment, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to these policies. Thank you for your understanding and cooperation.
